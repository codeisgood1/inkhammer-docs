What's new

What changed in each version. Your version is in Help > About Ink Hammer; Help > Check for updates says whether a newer one is out. Through the guide, a New in 0.9.24 tag marks what a version added.

0.9.24 New in 0.9.24

Cliffs, dunes and hills

  • Cliffs are lit by their real shape: ledges catch the light, risers turn with the sun (also by lamplight), and a shadow lies where the rock meets the ground on the shaded side. Sandstone strata and Earth bank light layer by layer. Cliffs, dunes and hills
  • New Breakup: the ground on top breaks onto the first ledges in patches, and the ground at the foot can creep up the lowest ones. Breakup
  • New Round top for dunes and banks: the side rolls over from the top like a dome. Round top
  • Cliff looks retuned: Earth slope is brown earth, Grassy hill is a low hill, the rock looks have breakup on, and a new cliff starts as warm grey painted rock. Cliffs already on your maps keep their look.
  • Cliffs and the spills over them take Opacity and Tint. A cliff that had a tint saved on it now shows it.
  • Dune and bank sides facing the sun are lit like the sand around them (every dune had a dark ring).

Props

  • New Submerge: sink a prop into the ground from any side, with a ragged soft edge and a touch of dirt. Submerge
  • A prop lying across the foot of a cliff no longer has a dark band cut across it.

Walls, buildings and roofs

  • Flip for walls, lines, buildings and roofs, and a whole selection as one piece. F flips a building or roof. Flip
  • Lift for walls: railings, beams and screens hang off the ground. Lift
  • Drawn roofs take Blend (Multiply, Add, Subtract); roof layers take every blend. Roof blend
  • Walls and buildings with Casts shadow off no longer throw shadows in the 3D lighting.

Water

  • The sea: waves keep their direction past ships and rocks, crests and troughs show in a still, a ship's wake fades into dashes. The sea looks are retuned to one painted style.
  • Rivers show which way they flow. Clear stream, Lake, Murky river and Swamp have the painted look; banks have light shallows and brush-stroke ripples.
  • Waterfalls break into falling strands, show a bright roll over the lip and take the colour of the water they pour into. Spills over cliffs land in smooth broken white water, and rocks show through the water running over them.

Selecting and Properties

  • A box drag picks everything it touches by its real shape.
  • With different kinds of things selected, Properties shows only the settings they all have. Every kind of thing shows its settings in the same order, and settings that do nothing for it are hidden.
  • A turned building's selection box turns with it. R and [ ] turn and scale a building like the mouse does.
  • Waterfalls, fire and cliffs are clicked near their centre line, so small falls stay easy to pick.
  • Properties gets most of the right column; the 3D view starts folded.

Speed and fixes

  • Maps open about a third faster.
  • Water no longer flashes white as a map opens, and the map no longer blinks a few seconds after opening.
  • Several undo fixes: a coast, a sea look change and the Fog command are each one Ctrl+Z.
