Using Ink Hammer

Walls and buildings

Single walls, fences and hedges come from the Walls tool or any Pen line. Whole buildings with rooms, doors and roofs come from the Build and Roofs tools. This page shows the wall styles and the newer ways to shape them.

Drawing walls

  • Walls tool (W): click to add points, double-click or Enter to finish. Walls edit like Pen lines: points, widths, curves.
  • Pick a style in the tool panel or in Properties. Height (ft) sets how tall it is for shadows and lighting; a 0 ft wall is a textured line that casts no shadow.
  • Doors and windows snap to a wall, cut their own opening and slide along it.
  • Wall kinds: normal, invisible (for the virtual tabletop only) and terrain (blocks sight, not movement). Walls view in the top bar shows what a virtual tabletop gets.

Wall styles

Every style in the Anvil Pack, drawn as the same corner with the afternoon sun from the top left.

Lift: walls off the ground New in 0.9.24

A railing along a walkway, a beam across a gap, a screen hanging from a ceiling: Properties > Height > Lift (ft) hangs a wall that many feet up. Its shadow lands apart from it and light passes under it, in both the flat and the 3D lighting. Height is still how tall the wall itself is.

Flip New in 0.9.24

Properties > Look > Flip has Left-right and Top-bottom buttons for walls, lines, buildings and roofs. With several things selected they flip as one piece across the middle of the selection: a house with its roof, walls and props mirrors together, and doors keep swinging the mirrored way. F flips a single building or roof.

Cliffs, waterfalls, spills and fire do not flip: their high side or the way they flow would swap.

Roof blend New in 0.9.24

A drawn roof (Roofs tool) has Blend in Properties > Look: Normal, Multiply, Add or Subtract. The roof keeps its sun and lamp shading. Multiply with a lower opacity makes a shadowy overhang that lets the floor and walls show through.

Screen, Overlay and the other blends are not offered for drawn roofs: a roof is lit as it is drawn, and those blends would light what is under it a second time. Roof layers (painted roofs) take every blend.

Selecting several things

  • A box drag picks everything the box touches, by its real shape.
  • With different kinds of things selected, Properties shows only the settings they all have.
  • Turning or scaling a building with R and [ ] works like the mouse: the walls keep their thickness, and a turned building's box turns with it.
