Troubleshooting

The usual problems and their fixes. Click a question to open it.

Lighting

I don't see any sun shadows
  • The sun switch at the top right (the first of the speed switches) may be off. The Lights panel says so and has a Show sun shadows button. See Speed switches.
  • In the Lights tool, check On and Cast shadows under Master light.
  • With Daylight at 100% shadows are faint. Lower it, or raise Shadow depth.
  • The object needs a height: props use Height (ft), and the layer needs Casts sun shadows.
A lamp does not light something

A lamp lights only its own layer and the layers under it. Drag the lamp's layer above the thing it should light, or move the thing to a lower layer. See Light and layer order.

Everything is dark inside a building

Buildings keep the sun out (as if roofed), so the inside only gets daylight and lamps. Add lamps inside, or select the building and untick Roofed for an open ruin.

Speed

The editor feels slow
  • View > Fast editing turns heavy effects off and draws at 50%. View > Full quality puts them back.
  • Or turn single speed switches off at the top right: animation and contact shade cost the most on big maps.
  • Lower the render scale (the % box at the top right). The map looks softer; exports are not affected.
  • The status bar shows frames per second and how long the map takes to draw, so you can see what helped.
The map looks soft or blurry

The render scale (top right) is below 100%, or Fast editing is on. Set it back to 100% or use View > Full quality.

Files

Ink Hammer closed and I lost my work

Start Ink Hammer again. If there is an autosaved copy newer than your file, it asks Recover unsaved work. Click Recover, then save. Ink Hammer autosaves every 2 minutes.

Your last saved version is also kept beside the map as a .bak file.

Art is missing when I open a map on another computer

Package the map on the first computer with File > Package map with its assets, then open the packaged copy.

Windows won't start Ink Hammer.exe

On the Windows protected your PC screen click More info, then Run anyway. The beta is not code-signed yet.

Reporting a bug

Help > Report a bug copies your Ink Hammer version, Windows version and graphics card to the clipboard. Paste them into your report with what you did just before it went wrong, and a screenshot if you can.
